Terms of Service

Last updated October 6, 2026. By creating an account, you agree to these terms.

QuietFox is in beta

QuietFox is currently in beta. Features may change, be added, or be removed during the beta, and the service may sometimes be unavailable or not work as expected. We'll tell you about major changes in the app.

Who can use QuietFox

You must be 18 or older and give your real date of birth. Each person may have one account, and each phone number can be linked to one account.

Peer support, not therapy

QuietFox connects you with other people for conversation. It isn't a medical, mental health, or emergency service, and people on QuietFox aren't counselors. If you're in crisis, use the Help button, call or text 988 in the US, or call your local emergency number.

Calls, messages, and privacy

Calls are never recorded. Messages are end-to-end encrypted, so QuietFox can't read them. If you report someone, the messages you choose to include are shared with our safety team. The Privacy Policy explains what we collect and why.

Following the Community guidelines

You agree to follow the Community guidelines. If you don't, we may warn you, suspend your account, or ban your account and phone number.

Reports, suspensions, and appeals

A serious report (sexual content, threats, or seeming to be under 18) can suspend your account right away. You can appeal once and return immediately, on probation. On probation, another serious report suspends your account until we review it. If we find that your appeal wasn't true, your account and phone number are permanently banned. For safety, matching may take longer for a while after a report, and account notices are sent on a schedule rather than right after a call.

Ending your account

You can delete your account at any time in Settings. We keep reports and safety records after an account is deleted, as the Privacy Policy explains.

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 7, 2026. QuietFox is in beta, and this policy may change as features change. We'll tell you about major changes in the app.

The short version

We collect what we need to run QuietFox and keep it safe. Calls are never recorded, and messages are end-to-end encrypted, so we can't listen to your calls or read your chats. We don't sell your information or show ads.

What we collect

  • Your account: email address, a scrambled (hashed) version of your password, date of birth, and phone number.
  • Your profile: display name, icon, short bio, and interests. Other people see these.
  • Call records: who you talked with, when, for how long, and how the call ended. Not what was said.
  • Things you choose to tell us: ratings, skip reasons, connection requests, reports, and feedback.
  • Messages: stored only in encrypted form that we can't unlock.
  • Safety records: reports about you and any warnings, suspensions, or bans.
  • Technical information: the last time you were active, your browser and device type when you send feedback, and the IP addresses our web server records in its logs.

What stays on your device

Your browser keeps your private encryption key, which unlocks your messages, and a login cookie. We never receive the private key. If you clear your browser data or switch devices, older messages can't be unlocked anymore.

Calls

Calls connect directly between the two browsers and are encrypted. To set up a call, the two browsers briefly exchange connection details, including network addresses, through our server. We delete those details as soon as the call ends.

Reports and notices

To protect people who report, notices about your account are sent once a day, at least 12 hours after a report, and matching may take longer for a short time after a report. You'll never see who reported you.

Why we use it

To run your account, check that everyone is 18 or older, match you for calls, keep people safe, review reports, prevent abuse like repeat accounts, fix bugs, and understand how QuietFox is used.

Reports

When you report someone from a chat, you can include recent messages. Your device unlocks them and sends them only to our safety team. The person you report isn't told who reported them.

Who we share it with

Only the services that help run QuietFox: a text message provider that sends your verification code (it receives your phone number), an email provider that sends password-reset links (it receives your email address), and our web host. We may share information if the law requires it, or if we believe someone's life is in danger.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in Settings. That removes your account details, profile, connections, and messages. We keep reports, safety records, and call records with your account link removed, and banned phone numbers stay banned.

Keeping it secure

Passwords are hashed, messages are end-to-end encrypted, and calls are encrypted. Encryption can't protect what's on your own screen, though: apps or browser extensions on your device that can read web pages may be able to see your chats once they're unlocked.

Adults only

QuietFox isn't for anyone under 18. If we learn an account belongs to someone under 18, we'll close it.

Community guidelines

QuietFox works because people treat each other gently. Breaking these rules can lead to a warning, a suspension, or a permanent ban.

Be kind

Listen without judging. No insults, threats, or hate based on who someone is.

No threats or violence

Never threaten anyone, encourage violence, or talk about hurting someone else.

Keep it clean

No sexual talk, no requests for photos, and no pressure to take a conversation anywhere else.

Protect your privacy, and theirs

Don't share or ask for real names, phone numbers, addresses, schools, workplaces, or social media accounts.

Adults only

Everyone must be 18 or older. If someone seems younger, end the call and report them as "Seems underage."

Peer support, not therapy

People here are friends, not counselors. If someone mentions hurting themselves, share the Help button and report with "I'm worried about this person" so our team can follow up.

No spam or selling

Don't advertise, promote your channels or pages, or ask people for money.

Don't record, screenshot, or stream

Never record, screenshot, or livestream a call or chat without the other person's clear permission. People come here expecting privacy.

Report honestly

Reports keep people safe, so use them only for real problems. False reports, joke reports, and reporting someone as revenge are violations too, and can lead to a warning or more.

Skip honestly

Skipping is fine. Picking a real reason helps us find people who make others uncomfortable.
